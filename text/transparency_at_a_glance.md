The summary tables provide a high-level snapshot of transparency across models in the dataset. Of the 956 models in the dataset (as of early July 2025), 87% were trained from scratch, while the remaining 11% were finetuned from existing base models. While the core analysis and visualisations focus exclusively on foundation models, basic analysis results for fine-tuned models are included here for additional context. The results reflect how often key development details were available in public metadata, based on the four dimensions used to calculate the transparency score.
